# OCI architecture diagram routing reference

Use the [`oci-diagramming` conventions](../skills/oci-diagramming/references/oci-architecture-conventions.md)
for Draw.io layers, official OCI stencils, boundaries, connector semantics,
observability pipelines, AI/GPU monitoring, security, multitenancy, and DR views.

Diagram creation and validation are offline artifact work. They do not require an
OCI context or tenancy preflight, and they must not be presented as live-topology
evidence unless a separately authorized and verified discovery step supplied the
sanitized source data.
