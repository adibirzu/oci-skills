# Nimb — OCI region pod

Nimb is a compact cloud region fused with a small data-center chassis: a steady infrastructure operator for OCI capability illustrations.

Style: **riso**
Aliases: cloud region, infrastructure pod, cloud mascot

## Locked design

- **Body**: one wide rounded infrastructure pod whose upper third forms an integrated three-lobed cloud crown and whose lower two-thirds form a sturdy rounded data-center chassis.
- **Rack core**: one charcoal arch-shaped inset centered on the lower torso with exactly three short horizontal cream slots, separated from the outer cream body.
- **Face**: two simple charcoal dot eyes high on the cream cloud crown, blank deadpan, no eyebrows, no mouth, and no other facial marks.
- **Accent carrier**: one Oracle-red rounded region-beacon arch rising behind the center cloud lobe — the only accent-colored part.
- Two short charcoal segmented cable arms ending in simple cream paddle hands; two short cream feet with charcoal soles.

## Interaction model

- Contact surfaces: cream paddle hands press, push, pat, and balance one nearby object; feet stand, brace, and press a pedal.
- Reach: short.
- Grip: pressure/contact only.
- Support/locomotion: two short feet and body mass when bracing.
- Protected regions: face interior — only the two dot eyes appear there.
